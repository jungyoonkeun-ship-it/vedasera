Shipping & Returns

This is the returns policy for all products sold by Veda Sera (주식회사 베다세라) through our official store. It is the same policy published on our Naver Smart Store product pages.

Shipping

  • Shipping within South Korea is free on all orders (no minimum).
  • Orders are delivered by CJ Logistics (CJ대한통운) in approximately 2–3 business days.

Change-of-mind returns

  • You may return an unused product for a change of mind within 7 days of receiving it.
  • Because outbound shipping was free, a return shipping fee of ₩8,000 applies to change-of-mind returns (the one-way return rate is ₩4,000; ₩8,000 is charged where the original delivery was free of charge).
  • The refund is issued after the returned product arrives and is inspected.

Defective or not-as-described products

  • If a product is defective or differs from its description, Veda Sera bears the return shipping cost.
  • Claims can be made within 3 months of receipt, or within 30 days of discovering the defect, whichever applies under Korean consumer law.
